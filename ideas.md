# LocalLens Redesign Directions

## Three Initial Directions

### Theme Name: Cartographic Editorial

**Very Brief Intro:** A civic-minded discovery workspace inspired by city atlases, field journals, and modern wayfinding. Search results should feel like gaining context about a neighborhood rather than browsing a generic directory.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Sunlit Market Ledger

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm storefront-forward direction influenced by independent shop signs, printed receipts, and weekend market ephemera. It puts tangible local commerce and small-business character at the center.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Signal After Dark

**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast city-at-night system using clear activity signals and sharp information surfaces. It makes real-time discovery feel immediate and energetic.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction: Cartographic Editorial

### Design Movement

The redesign follows **contemporary cartographic editorialism**, combining the orderly visual logic of a city atlas with the pacing and character of an independent travel journal.

### Core Principles

1. Place before interface: spatial context leads every discovery task.
2. Layered clarity: a dark atlas rail, paper-toned workspace, and restrained cards create hierarchy.
3. Useful texture: contour lines, coordinate labels, and data rules support comprehension rather than decoration.
4. Human-scale discovery: each business is understood through category, availability, distance, and detail.

### Color Philosophy

Map-paper ivory creates a calm reading surface; deep ink gives navigation and key type authority; **Verdigris Teal (`#0E7C78`)** denotes active place intelligence; signal coral is reserved for alerts, saves, and timely states.

### Layout Paradigm

Desktop views follow a field-desk composition: an atlas navigation rail beside an editorial discovery column and a contextual map pane. On small screens, map context moves to a dedicated destination while the discovery feed remains primary.

### Signature Elements

Coordinate chips, subtle contour-line textures, and editorial index numerals make every major surface recognizably LocalLens.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like repositioning a map pin: direct, contextual, and reversible. Selection synchronizes the result list and map context without hiding the user’s escape routes.

### Animation

Use short location-oriented motion: 160ms selection changes, 220ms drawer and panel reveals, and 40ms list-entry staggering. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all nonessential movement.

### Typography System

DM Serif Display gives major headings an editorial point of view, Manrope keeps reading and controls calm, and IBM Plex Mono is used only for coordinates, distances, and metadata.

### Brand Essence

**LocalLens is the field guide for people who want to understand and choose their nearby city with more confidence.** Its personality is observant, grounded, and discerning.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are precise and place-led; actions invite exploration without generic urgency; microcopy makes filters and location states explicit. Example headline: “Find the places your neighborhood keeps returning to.” Example action: “Open the local field guide.”

### Wordmark and Logo

The mark is a compact lens-and-map-pin glyph: a circular viewing aperture interrupted by a precise location point and offset contour arc. It contains no text and must remain distinct at favicon scale.
